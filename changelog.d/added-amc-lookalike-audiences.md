- **Build an AMC lookalike audience and move a DSP line item onto it.** Ask the
  Amazon Marketing Cloud skill for a lookalike of a seed audience, such as your
  high-value buyers, and it builds one: the seed query picks the shoppers to
  resemble, you choose how close or how broad (Most similar, Similar, Balanced,
  Broad or Most broad, the same choices as the AMC console), and Amazon models
  new shoppers like them and activates the audience on your DSP advertiser. You
  see a preview first and confirm, as with other audience changes. A lookalike's
  seed covers at most the last 90 days, the AMC limit, and the preview says when
  that shortens your seed. Once the lookalike is ready, the plugin can swap it
  onto a line item in place of the seed audience, keep your exclusions, and set
  the line item to target exactly the audiences you chose, so DSP does not widen
  the lookalike again. The skill also explains how DSP combines included and
  excluded audiences on a line item.
