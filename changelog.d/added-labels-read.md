- **Read the labels on your items and campaigns from the plugin.** `mixshift labels` shows the labels you put on your items (ASINs) and Amazon campaigns to organize them: Item Group, Brand, Nickname, Parent ASIN, Item Label 1 and 2, Tags 1 to 4, and for campaigns Manager and Objective. `labels state` says whether labels are on for your account and how fresh the copy is, `labels fields` lists the fields and the values in use, `labels get` looks up specific ASINs or campaign ids (any number of them), `labels search` lists items or campaigns a page at a time or only those with a given value, `labels drift` shows where your Data Manager rows disagree, and `labels changes` is a feed of what changed, oldest first. An id MixShift has not read yet shows as never seen, which is different from an item with no label set. It only reads: nothing here changes a label. Labels are being turned on account by account; until they are on for yours, each command says so in one line. While a copy is being kept current from the Data Manager, an answer can be up to 15 minutes behind a label someone just edited there.
