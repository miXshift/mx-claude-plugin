- **Keyword rank over time, for the keywords MixShift tracks for you.** MixShift can now track keywords for your organization: once a day it takes the Amazon search results for each tracked keyword at each tracked shopper location, on desktop and mobile, and keeps every position. `mixshift shelf rank-history "<keyword>" --zip <zip>` reads that history back, free: per day and ASIN the absolute rank, the organic rank and the sponsored rank, with the latest position and the movement per ASIN at the top. Only keywords, locations and views MixShift tracks for you answer; for anything else the Amazon Shelf skill offers to send the tracking request ("track these keywords"). Tracking is set up by MixShift on request, and the daily observations count against the monthly shelf allowance like any other answer.
