- **Claude builds Sponsored Brands product collection ads the way Amazon now accepts them.** Claude creates the ad group, then the ad, and only then its keywords or targets, since Amazon can refuse targets in an ad group that has no ad yet. Each ad needs at least 3 products and a headline of 32 characters or fewer, and it can land on your Store or on a product list that needs no Store, but not on a product detail page. Custom images are left out: Amazon accepts them on this ad type and then drops them without saying so, so Claude tells you an image cannot be added this way. The preview now flags too few products, a long headline, a product detail landing page and a custom image before anything reaches Amazon.
