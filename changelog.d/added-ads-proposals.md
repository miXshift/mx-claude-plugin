- **Ads changes can now be proposed now and approved later.** Add `--propose` to a write such as `mixshift ads call sp.update_campaigns` and the change set is saved as a proposal instead of being applied, with a title, a rationale and optional evidence for whoever approves it. A signed-in person reviews it with `mixshift ads proposals show`, approves it with `mixshift ads proposals approve` (a money change over 25% needs their explicit acknowledgment), and only then does `mixshift ads proposals commit` send it to Amazon. A proposal expires if nobody acts, and is refused rather than applied if the live values changed since it was written. This is the way for a scheduled routine to suggest budget and bid changes without ever making them.
