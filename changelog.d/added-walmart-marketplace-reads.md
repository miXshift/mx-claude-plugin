- **Walmart Marketplace in the plugin: `mx-walmart-retail`, read only.** For sellers connected to your MixShift account, ask what is selling, what you have, what is inbound, how many days it will last and what to reorder, and get one line per SKU with the riskiest first. It also reads orders by date window, items, inventory and inbound shipments, straight from your Walmart seller account. A Seller Center admin connects the seller on the web and can disconnect at any time. It never changes anything on Walmart: price, inventory and order changes are made in the MixShift app. The commands are `mixshift walmart marketplace sellers`, `operations` and `call`.
