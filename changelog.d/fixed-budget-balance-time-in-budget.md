- **Budget Balance no longer recommends a raise for campaigns that had budget left over.** It read the time-in-budget figure Amazon reports backwards: a campaign that stayed within its budget most of the week could count as budget-capped and land in the raise list, and one that ran out of budget most of the week missed that signal. A campaign now counts as capped on that figure only when it stayed in budget less than half of the last 7 days, and its reason says so ("in budget only 30 percent of the last 7 days"). Earlier raise lists may have included campaigns that were not budget-capped: before acting on one, check the raises whose reason cited time in budget rather than days at 90 percent of budget.
