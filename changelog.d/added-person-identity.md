- **`mixshift auth status` and `mixshift doctor` now show who you are signed in as.** One line gives your email and your organization, and when you signed in as yourself with Google it says "verified member" with your role. Anything your organization does not allow you to do is listed. `auth status` is read-only: it uses your saved sign-in as it is, never renews it, and while it is expired says the identity will show after your next MixShift call. `doctor` checks that your sign-in works, so it may renew it, as before. If the service is slow or unreachable they give up after a few seconds and say so. When a request is refused for a missing permission, the message now says the permission may be off for you in your organization and to ask your org admin (a service credential has to be granted it when the credential is created), instead of saying signed-in users always hold it; if the service says why, the message names the cause. The device sign-in shows the code as its own step, and the work email you give at sign-in is now a prefill hint for the sign-in page, not your identity.
