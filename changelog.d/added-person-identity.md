- **`mixshift auth status` and `mixshift doctor` now show who you are signed in as.** One line gives your email, your organization and role, and whether your session is verified (you signed in as yourself) or only a typed email on a shared login, plus anything your organization does not allow you to do. When your organization has not turned on member sign-in, or the service is older, the line says what it can and never guesses. If the service cannot be reached, status still works and says the identity could not be read. When a request is refused for a missing permission, the message now says it may be off for you in your organization and to ask your org admin, instead of saying signed-in users always hold it. The work email you give at sign-in is now a prefill hint for the sign-in page, not your identity.
