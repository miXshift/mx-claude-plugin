- **Manage your rank tracking from the plugin.** `mixshift shelf tracking` lists the keywords your organization tracks, with each set's calls a day and the projected monthly use against your shelf allowance. Owners, admins and managers can create a tracking set, add and remove tracked keywords and ASINs, and pause, resume or delete a set; in chat, say "show my tracked keywords" or "add trail mix to my rank tracking". Every running set uses keywords x zips x views x pages of the monthly shelf allowance each day, and a change that would take the projected use past the allowance is refused with both numbers; reading the rank history stays free. Anyone else can still ask MixShift to track a keyword.
