- **ASIN target negation review counts each location's lifetime results once.**
  The same product-target search-term rows can be stored in two places, and the
  review used to add both together, so lifetime spend, sales, orders and clicks
  at a campaign and ad group could show at up to twice their real value.
  Doubled orders could keep a losing ASIN target above the lifetime-orders
  floor and off the persistent-loser list. Lifetime figures now come from one
  source.
