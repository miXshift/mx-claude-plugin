- **Read and change the labels on your items and campaigns by asking Claude.** Labels are the fields you use to organize your items (ASINs) and Amazon campaigns: Item Group, Brand, Nickname, Parent ASIN, Item Label 1 and 2, Tags 1 to 4, and for campaigns Manager and Objective. Ask things like "Using MixShift, which item label fields do we have, and what is each one named?" or "Using MixShift, set Item Label 1 to Spring for these ASINs". Claude looks the labels up first, then shows a preview of exactly what would change, and nothing changes until you say yes. Every change records who made it and can be undone, and the Legacy platform's screens show it within a few minutes. An item change reaches every marketplace of the merchant by default; a campaign change reaches its one marketplace. One change can cover up to 1,000 items or campaigns. Scripts and automations can do the same with the `mixshift labels` commands, the REST API or the MCP tools. Labels are being turned on account by account. If yours is not on yet, say "Using MixShift, ask to turn on labels for our account" and MixShift will turn them on and let you know. The guide is at https://mixshift.ai/knowledge-base/labels.
