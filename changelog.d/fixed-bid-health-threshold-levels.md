- **Keyword bid health no longer flags keywords under your ACoS target for a bid cut because of how the thresholds were described.** The scale and pullback thresholds are ACoS levels: a pullback of 45 means keywords above 45% ACoS. The brand template used to describe them as a percent above or below your target and filled both in at 50, so brands that followed it could see many keywords under target listed for a cut. The template now describes them as ACoS levels and leaves them unset, so the skill uses your target x 0.7 to scale and x 1.5 to pull back. When saved thresholds look like offsets (scale at or above pullback, or pullback below your target), the calibration card shows a warning and asks you once to fix them or keep them. Your values are never changed for you.
