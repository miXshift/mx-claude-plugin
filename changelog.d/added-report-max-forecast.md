- **Monthly Performance Report Max can show your forecast.** When a forecast has been
  published for the brand in the MixShift forecasting service and covers the month being
  reported, the report adds it on its own: the client brief compares the month with what was
  forecast before it closed, and the internal companion adds what the forecast stands on and
  the current model's view. A brand with no current forecast gets exactly the report it got
  before, with nothing forecast-related in it. Turn it off, keep it internal, or pick a
  sub-brand with the new forecast settings.
