- **Sponsored Brands batches now follow the real per-call limits.** Creating or
  changing Sponsored Brands campaigns, ad groups and ads takes at most 10 items
  per call, and keywords, negative keywords and product targets take at most 100.
  The guidance used to say 200, so a larger batch could be turned down in full
  with nothing created. A batch over the limit is now stopped before anything is
  sent, with the limit named, so you can split it and send it again.
