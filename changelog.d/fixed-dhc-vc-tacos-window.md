- **The daily health check no longer overstates TACoS for Vendor Central accounts.** Vendor Central sales arrive a few days late, and how late varies. The check used to assume a fixed delay, so on many days it compared ad spend with sales from different days, and month-to-date counted spend for days whose sales had not arrived yet. Now ad spend and sales for the 1-day, 7-day, 30-day and month-to-date windows cover the same days, each ending on the latest day with Vendor Central sales. Seller Central reports are unchanged.
