- **Rank history now tells "not in the top N" apart from a missed day.** `mixshift shelf rank-history` used to show nothing on a day your product held no position in the stored pages, which looked like a skipped observation even though every tracked keyword is observed daily. Each ASIN now shows how many observed days it ranked on, lists the days it was observed but not in the top N (N is how deep that day's results went), and names any day with no snapshot as "no observation"; ask "was it not ranking, or did you miss a day?" and the answer says which. These lines appear once the MixShift service starts sending the observed days; until then the output is unchanged.
